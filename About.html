OUR HISTORY! 📜

💡
2025 January

The Spark

Founded in 2025, JJ PLV began as a shared idea among a group of students constantly frustrated by the lack of affordable, high-quality, and aesthetic stationery. We realized everyday study tools were either too expensive or lacked personality.

🚀
2025 June

Official Launch

JJ PLV officially opened! We believe putting pen to paper is a magical experience! What started as a simple obsession to find and design fun, functional, and affordable stationery quickly grew into a full-fledged business.

✨
2026

Expansion

Today, JJ PLV offers a curated range designed to keep you organized, focused, and inspired. Whether you're journaling or studying, we bring joy to your everyday tasks! Many students who use our stationery leave glowing feedback, and our monthly sales continue to grow as more people discover our products.
